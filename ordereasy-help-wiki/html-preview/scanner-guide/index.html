Scanner app guide

The Retailer Scanner is a mobile app for shop staff who need to add many products quickly — by scanning barcodes and photographing packs.

What the scanner is (and is not)

Scanner is…Scanner is not…
A fast capture tool at the shelfWhere you edit your full catalog
A way to upload sessions to the cloudThe POS, label printer, or order app
Used with same login as retailer appA customer-facing app

Review and publish always happens in the retailer web app under Products → Bulk Add.

Who should use it

  • Owner setting up catalog for the first time
  • Staff during annual stock-take
  • Helper walking aisles while manager reviews on laptop

Requirements

  • Android phone with camera
  • Retailer login credentials
  • Internet to sync sessions
  • Scanner app installed (provided during shop onboarding)
