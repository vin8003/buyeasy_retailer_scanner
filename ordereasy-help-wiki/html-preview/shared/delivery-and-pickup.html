Delivery and pickup

Shops choose how customers receive orders. OrderEasy supports both — but does not provide delivery staff.

Pickup

Customer comes to the shop to collect a ready bag.

AspectDetail
Best forNearby regulars, no delivery staff
Shop effortPack and notify when ready
Customer effortTravel to shop
AddressShop location shown in app

Pickup flow

  1. Customer selects pickup at checkout
  2. Shop prepares order
  3. Shop marks ready / delivered when customer collects
  4. Customer pays COD/UPI at counter if not prepaid

Delivery

Shop sends order to customer’s address (within rules they set).

AspectDetail
Best forWider reach, convenience
Shop effortPack + assign staff/rider you employ
Customer effortWait at address
AddressCustomer enters delivery address

Delivery flow

  1. Customer selects delivery and enters address
  2. Shop checks address is inside delivery radius
  3. Shop may charge delivery fee or require minimum order
  4. Shop packs and marks out for delivery
  5. Shop marks delivered when handover complete

What shops configure

In Profile / shop settings:

SettingExample
Offer delivery?Yes / No
Offer pickup?Yes / No
Delivery radius2 km from shop
Minimum order for delivery₹200
Delivery charge₹30 flat

Be honest in settings — customers see these rules at checkout.

What OrderEasy does not do

  • No OrderEasy rider fleet
  • No live map tracking of third-party couriers
  • No automatic route optimisation

Logistics are your shop’s responsibility, like phone orders always were.

Choosing a mode as customer

At checkout you only see what the shop supports. If delivery is greyed out:

  • You may be outside radius
  • Shop may be pickup-only
  • Try pickup or contact shop

Tips for retailers

TipReason
Start with pickup onlySimpler when going online first
Add delivery when staff availableAvoid late deliveries
Clear minimum orderCovers delivery cost
Call if address unclearSaves failed trips
