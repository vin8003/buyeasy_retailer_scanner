Track your order

After you place an order, you can follow its progress in the Orders section of the customer app.

Where to find your orders

  1. Open customer.ordereasy.win
  2. Go to Orders (from profile or main menu)
  3. Tap an order to see details

Order detail shows:

  • Items and quantities
  • Total amount
  • Delivery address or pickup info
  • Current status
  • Option to chat with the shop
  • Option to rate after delivery

What each status means

StatusWhat is happening
PendingShop received your order; waiting for them to accept
ConfirmedShop accepted — they will prepare it
ProcessingShop is picking and packing items
PackedOrder is ready — next step depends on delivery or pickup
Out for deliveryOn the way to your address (delivery orders)
Ready for pickupCome to the shop to collect (pickup orders)
DeliveredOrder complete
CancelledOrder was cancelled (by you or shop)
Waiting for your approvalShop changed something — you must accept or reject

For a full explanation, see Order statuses explained.

Notifications

You may receive push notifications on your phone when:

  • Shop confirms your order
  • Status changes (packed, out for delivery, delivered)
  • Shop sends a message
  • Shop modified your order and needs your approval

Allow notifications when prompted for the best experience.

When the shop changes your order

Sometimes an item is out of stock or the shop needs to adjust quantity. They may modify your order.

You will see Waiting for your approval:

  • Accept — order continues with the changes
  • Reject — order may be cancelled or revised

Review changes carefully before accepting.

Chat with the shop

Use order chat for questions about:

  • Substitutions
  • Delivery timing
  • Missing items
  • Special requests

Keep messages related to the order. For urgent issues, a phone call to the shop may still be faster.

After delivery

Once status is Delivered:

  • Check items against your order
  • Pay if COD was selected and not yet paid
  • Leave a rating or review if you wish — it helps the shop and other customers

Something wrong?

SituationSuggested action
Order stuck on Pending long timeMessage shop via chat or call them
Wrong items receivedContact shop immediately via chat
Never arrivedCheck status; contact shop; use Support in profile if needed
Want to cancelCancel only if still Pending — after confirm, ask shop via chat
