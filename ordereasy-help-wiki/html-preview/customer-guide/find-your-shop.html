Find your shop

OrderEasy shows you local shops you can order from. You pick one shop and shop only from their catalog.

Allow location (recommended)

When you open the customer app, it asks you to confirm where you are (GPS or city) before you shop. That keeps the shop list honest.

Say yes to location if you are comfortable — this is the fastest way to see shops near you.

What happens: The app matches you to a service city and shows shops there, with delivery/pickup options for your area.

Pick a city manually

If you decline location or GPS is unavailable:

  1. You will be asked to select your state, then city (India list)
  2. The app may guess city from your network so you can confirm instead of typing
  3. The app shows shops registered in that area
  4. You can still order — distance may be less precise

GPS without a city/pincode is not enough to list shops. Confirm the city even if the pin looks right.

The shop list

Each shop card typically shows:

InfoWhat it tells you
Shop nameWhich kirana you are looking at
DistanceHow far from you (when location is on)
RatingFeedback from other customers
Delivery / Pickup badgesWhat this shop offers
Minimum orderIf the shop set one for delivery

Tap a shop to enter their storefront.

Choosing the right shop

OrderEasy is not a marketplace — you are not comparing prices across fifty shops on one cart. You choose one shop per order.

Tips:

  • Pick the shop you already buy from — that is who OrderEasy is designed for
  • Check if they offer delivery to your address or pickup only
  • Look at operating hours — some shops may not accept orders outside open hours

Inside the shop

Once you select a shop, you see only that shop’s products:

  • Categories (snacks, staples, household, etc.)
  • Search within this catalog
  • Product details with live price and availability

To order from a different shop, go back to the shop list and switch — your cart is per shop.
