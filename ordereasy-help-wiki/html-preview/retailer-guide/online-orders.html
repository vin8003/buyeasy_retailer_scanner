Online orders

When customers order through customer.ordereasy.win, orders appear in your Orders queue. This page explains how to handle them from receipt to delivery.

Where orders appear

Open Orders from the sidebar. You will see lists such as:

  • Pending — new orders needing your response
  • In progress — confirmed and being prepared
  • Returned — sales returns and fully returned orders
  • Completed — delivered or picked up
  • Cancelled — did not go through

You may also get a push notification when a new order arrives.

Step 1: Review pending order

Tap a pending order to see:

  • Customer name and contact
  • Delivery address or pickup
  • Each item, quantity, and price
  • Payment method (COD, UPI, etc.)
  • Special instructions

Check you have stock and can fulfil before confirming.

Step 2: Confirm, modify, or cancel

ActionWhen to use
ConfirmYou can fulfil as requested
ModifyItem unavailable or quantity must change — customer must approve
CancelCannot fulfil at all — explain in message if possible

If you modify an order

Status becomes Waiting for customer approval. The customer must accept or reject in their app. Do not pack until they accept.

Step 3: Prepare the order

After confirmation, advance status as you work:

StatusMeaning for staff
ProcessingPicking items from shelf
PackedBagged and ready

Update status so customers know progress — fewer phone calls.

Step 4: Hand over

Delivery modeFinal steps
DeliveryMark Out for delivery when rider/staff leaves → Delivered when customer receives
PickupTell customer to collect → mark Delivered when they pick up

There is no OrderEasy rider app — your staff or you handle delivery.

Chat with customer

Use order chat for:

  • "Dal brand X out of stock — OK with brand Y?"
  • "Delivery in 30 minutes"
  • Clarifying address or landmark

Payment on delivery

For COD orders, collect payment when delivering or at pickup. Mark paid according to your shop process.

For UPI, customer may pay on receipt — confirm in your usual way.

Cancelling after confirm

Avoid if possible. If you must cancel after confirming, contact customer via chat and cancel in system — affects their trust and your ratings.

Daily workflow suggestion

Open shop → Check Pending → Confirm all you can → Process in order received
→ Pack → Dispatch or ready for pickup → Mark Delivered → End of day: clear stragglers
