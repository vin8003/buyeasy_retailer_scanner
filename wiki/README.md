# OrderEasy Help Wiki

Welcome to the OrderEasy help centre. This wiki explains how the platform works for **shop owners** and **customers** — in plain language, end to end.

OrderEasy is software for a **single neighbourhood kirana or local shop** in India. It is **not** a marketplace and **not** a delivery company. One shop runs its counter, stock, supplier accounts, and online orders from customers who already know that shop.

Last reviewed against the live apps: **18 Sep 2026**. See [What’s new](whats-new.md) for what changed since this wiki was first written.
